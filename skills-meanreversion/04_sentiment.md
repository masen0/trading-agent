# Sentiment Analysis — Contrarian Core

**KEY CONCEPT — sentiment is a *primary* signal here, not a minor input.** Mean-reversion and contrarian sentiment are the same idea expressed two ways: extremes revert. **Peak fear marks bounce-worthy bottoms; peak euphoria marks exhausted tops.** Where the trend-following book treated sentiment as low-weight noise, here it is weighted heavily — it independently confirms that a price extreme is an *emotional* extreme, which is exactly what reverts.

---

## 1. Retail / Social Sentiment (contrarian at extremes)

Search Reddit (r/wallstreetbets, r/stocks), X (`$TICKER`), StockTwits.

| Signal | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| **Panic / capitulation** ("it's over", "I sold everything", flood of doom posts) | **Strong buy confirmation** — retail capitulation clusters near local bottoms |
| Fear + heavy volume down day | Supportive — emotion is stretched |
| Euphoria ("to the moon", FOMO, everyone bullish) | **Exit/fade confirmation** — crowded, exhausted |
| Quiet / neutral | No edge |

The more emotional and one-sided the crowd, the stronger the contrarian signal. Substance doesn't matter here — *intensity and one-sidedness* do.

---

## 2. Fear/Greed & Volatility

- **VIX spiking** into a down move = fear premium rising → bounce fuel building (but wait for the reversal bar; a rising VIX alone isn't a bottom).
- **Put/call ratio elevated** (heavy put buying) = defensive extreme → contrarian positive.
- **IV Rank high** on the name after a drop = fear priced in → mean reversion of both price *and* volatility often follows.

---

## 3. Analyst / Options Flow (secondary)

| Signal | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| A cluster of downgrades *after* a big drop | Often the capitulation marker — analysts chase price; the bottom is frequently near |
| Panic put sweeps into support | Contrarian positive — hedging peaks near lows |
| Broad euphoric call chasing at highs | Contrarian negative — fade |

**Note:** analyst herding is a *contrarian* tell here — they downgrade near lows and upgrade near highs, the opposite of what helps them.

---

## Distinguishing "buyable fear" from "justified fear"

Contrarian sentiment only works when the fear is **emotional, not informational**. Cross-check with `05`:

- Fear with **no thesis-breaking news** (broad risk-off, sector rotation, "sell the news" on a *beat*) → **buyable** emotional extreme.
- Fear driven by a **real negative event** (guidance cut, fraud, investigation, secular disruption) → **justified**, not a reversion setup. The crowd is right. Do not fade it.

## Sentiment Score (0–5)

| Score | Conditions |
|---|---|
| 5 | Clear retail capitulation + VIX spike + put extreme, with NO thesis-breaking news |
| 4 | Strong fear signals at support, news-clean |
| 3 | Mild fear / mixed |
| 2 | Neutral — no emotional extreme (little reversion edge) |
| 1 | Emerging euphoria (fade territory for exits, not a long) |
| 0 | Fear is justified by real bad news — not a contrarian setup |
