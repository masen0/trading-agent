# News & Macro — The Falling-Knife Filter

**KEY CONCEPT — news is used to answer ONE question: *why* is this stock down?** A dip caused by noise (broad risk-off, rotation, "sell the news" on good numbers) reverts and is buyable. A dip caused by a **real, thesis-changing event** does not revert on your timeframe — the price has been repriced for a reason, and the crowd is correct. This file is the veto that keeps dip-buying from becoming knife-catching.

---

## Classify every dip before buying it

For any oversold candidate, find the cause of the drop and classify it:

| Cause of the drop | Class | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Broad market / sector risk-off day (SPY, SMH red; nothing company-specific) | **Technical** | Buyable if setup qualifies |
| Sector rotation (money leaving the group short-term) | **Technical** | Buyable |
| "Sell the news" after a **beat-and-raise** | **Technical** | Buyable — fundamentals improved, price overshot down |
| Profit-taking after an extended run | **Technical** | Buyable at support |
| Sympathy move (a *peer's* bad news drags the name) | **Usually technical** | Buyable if the name itself is unaffected |
| **Guidance cut / earnings miss** on THIS name | **Fundamental** | **Do not buy** — repriced |
| **Investigation, fraud, litigation, regulatory hit** | **Fundamental** | **Do not buy** |
| **Secular/competitive disruption** (product obsoleted, key customer lost) | **Fundamental** | **Do not buy** |
| **Dilution / capital distress** | **Fundamental** | **Do not buy** |

Rule of thumb: **if the reason the stock dropped is specific to the company and changes its future, it is not a dip — it is a new, lower fair value.**

---

## Earnings — hard avoidance

- **Do NOT open a dip-buy within 3 trading days of the company's earnings.** A binary event dwarfs the reversion edge, and a post-earnings gap is the most common way a "cheap" stock gets cheaper.
- **Mandatory per-session earnings check for all current holdings** (use the earnings-calendar tool). Flag any holding reporting within 3 sessions; do not add, and be ready for gap risk.
- A post-earnings **beat-and-raise that sold off** is one of the best reversion setups — the fundamentals improved while the price fell. Prioritize these.

---

## Macro Context (light)

Mean-reversion is short-horizon, so macro matters mainly as a *regime and timing* input:

- **Risk-off macro day (broad selloff, no single catalyst)** → generates the best broad oversold baskets; buy the strongest names at support.
- **Scheduled event risk today** (FOMC, CPI print, major jobs report) → reversion setups are unreliable into the print; wait until after.
- **Sustained macro deterioration** (rates spiking, credit stress) → can turn a range into a downtrend; respect the 200-SMA gate, which will start failing names first.

## News Score (0–5)

| Score | Conditions |
|---|---|
| 5 | Dip is clearly technical (broad/rotation/sell-the-news on a beat), name news-clean, no near earnings |
| 4 | Technical dip, minor/no news |
| 3 | Ambiguous cause — needs the sentiment/technical picture to break the tie |
| 2 | Some company-specific concern, not clearly thesis-breaking — caution |
| 1 | Near earnings (within 3 days) OR unresolved company-specific worry |
| 0 | Thesis-breaking event — falling knife, veto the trade |
