# Fundamental Analysis (Light Filter)

**KEY CONCEPT — for mean-reversion, fundamentals are a *filter, not a driver*.** You are not investing in the business; you are renting a bounce over days. Fundamentals matter only to answer one question: **is this a sound enough company that an oversold dip is likely to be bought back, rather than the start of a justified collapse?** Deep valuation work is wasted here — a quick quality gate is enough.

---

## The Quality Gate (pass/fail, ~2 minutes)

Before dip-buying any name, confirm it is NOT a deteriorating business. Quick checks:

| Check | Source | Pass condition |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue trend | Web search / fundamentals | Growing or stable YoY (not in decline) |
| Profitability / cash | Web search | Profitable or clearly funded (not cash-burning toward distress) |
| Recent earnings | Web search | No miss-and-guide-down in the last report |
| Balance sheet | Web search | No solvency red flags (debt crisis, going-concern) |

- **All pass** → the name is a valid reversion candidate; the dip is likely technical and buyable.
- **Any fail** → **do not dip-buy.** A weak business that is falling may keep falling for good reason. This is the fundamental half of the falling-knife filter (`05` covers the news half).

---

## What NOT to do here

- Do **not** build a full DCF, PEG, or multi-quarter margin analysis — the holding period is days, not quarters.
- Do **not** let a great fundamental story tempt you into buying a dip that is *below the 200-day SMA*. Quality does not override the trend filter (`03`). A great company in a technical downtrend is still a falling knife on this timeframe.
- Do **not** dip-buy within 3 trading days of the company's earnings — binary event risk overwhelms the reversion edge (`05`).

---

## Fundamental Gate Score (0–2, feeds the composite)

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 2 | Clearly healthy business — dip is almost certainly technical |
| 1 | Adequate — no red flags, some mixed signals |
| 0 | Fails the gate — do not dip-buy regardless of how oversold |

This is a smaller weight than in the trend-following book on purpose: technicals (`03`) carry the decision here.
